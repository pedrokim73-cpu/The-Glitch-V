About the Competition

What is
The Glitch?

A fast-paced logic competition where problems aren't what they seem. Every puzzle is designed to break your assumptions — and reward creative thinking.

01 //
Fast-Paced

Two hours. Multiple challenges. No time to overthink — your instincts and logic are your tools.

02 //
Puzzle-Heavy

Problems built around pattern recognition, deduction, and lateral thinking — not rote formulas.

03 //
Creative Over Memory

You won't need to memorize theorems. You'll need to think differently when the system glitches.

⚡ 5th annual edition  ·  Organized by THOTH Club (ASFM Math Club)
Open to all ASFM high school students  ·  Teams of 2–3  ·  12 teams max

Format & Rules

How it
Works

Station-based. Two hours. No excuses. Everything you need to know to feel confident walking in.

Format
  • Station-based — teams rotate every 15 minutes
  • At most 2 teams per station at any time
  • Single-round competition (2 hours total)
  • Logic and deduction emphasis
  • No advanced math — algebra at most
Rules
  • Collaboration within your team only
  • No outside help or external resources
  • No calculators — logic & basic computation only
  • Teams of 2–3 members · 12 team limit
  • All ASFM high school students eligible
Content Level
  • Creative & lateral thinking problems
  • Pattern recognition challenges
  • Logic deduction sequences
  • "Glitch-style" trick questions
  • Mixed grade levels welcome
Scoring
  • Points awarded per problem solved
  • Tie-breaker system in place
  • Top 3 teams take home prizes
  • All participants receive free lunch
  • Winners receive exclusive merch

FAQ

Last-Minute
Questions

Do I need to be good at math? ∨
No — over the years, the math level of participants had no significant correlation to the winning teams. For example, in the third edition of The Glitch (2024), a team of 9th graders won the second-place prize.
Can I participate alone? ∨
No — The Glitch traditionally celebrates teamwork, for which the participants must have a team.
Can teams be mixed grades? ∨
Yes — you are free to form teams with whoever you want, as long as they attend ASFM high school.
What should I bring? ∨
You don't need to bring anything — all necessary materials will be provided for you.
Are calculators allowed? ∨
No — and they are not useful for this competition, as there is only simple arithmetic and some algebra involved.
How hard is it, really? ∨
We wouldn't describe this competition as "hard," but more of a test for pattern recognition, lateral thinking, and creativity.
When is the registration deadline? ∨
Register no later than April 30th, 2026.
